# Ideas and kitchen notes

Not part of the site build. Recipes move into `recipes.json` only after they've been made
and were good enough to make again.

## To try

- **Red chile beef tamales with roasted tomatillo salsa.** Next big project. Chuck for the
  filling, lard in the masa. Salsa: broiled tomatillos, serranos, cilantro, lime, chicken
  bouillon powder. First real attempt at steaming anything.
- **Tikka masala birria.** Yogurt-marinated, charred beef in a tikka masala direction, still
  beef, keeping the consommé. Interactive recipe page already exists as a Claude artifact
  ("Tikka Masala Birria"). Worry: 6 lb of meat and a full day if it misses. Consider a
  small test batch first.
- **Corn tortillas from scratch.** Masa harina, warm water, salt. Goes with either birria.
- **Cottage cheese pimento cheese.** 1 cup 4% Mootopia cottage cheese blended smooth, 4 oz
  sharp cheddar (the post-oak smoked one), 4 oz jar diced pimentos, 1 tbsp Duke's, garlic
  powder, smoked paprika, hot sauce. Make it thick. For smash burgers in place of the cheese.
- **Almond and oat flour pancakes, with Nurri.** The version from over a year ago beat regular
  pancakes; Kodiak mix didn't scratch the itch. Exact recipe is lost and needs rebuilding.
  Half Nurri, half 2% milk as the liquid worked in the Kodiak batch.
- **Butter chicken empanadas.** Goya discs, curry reduced until jammy, chicken chopped fine,
  cooled, air fried at 375°F for 8 to 10 minutes. Never made; the butter chicken ran out first.
- **Birria consommé focaccia.** Consommé as the liquid, skimmed birria fat, shredded meat and
  Oaxaca on top. Never made; the consommé went off first. Freeze consommé in portions next time.
- **Tomato bisque butter chicken.** Costco tomato soup as the sauce base, protein milk in
  place of cream.
- Smaller ones: arayes with homemade pita, keema samosas, vindaloo with chuck.

## Needs a rework before it earns a page

- **Butter chicken pasta** (Oct 2026). Frozen tenders, MDH butter chicken masala, Barami
  protein pasta, evaporated milk. Fine, but less enjoyable than plain pasta or butter chicken
  with naan.

## Made, not adding

- **Tuna salad** (Nov 2026). Safe Catch, light sour cream, light mayo, dill, lime, Dijon, hot
  sauce. Good, nothing unique to it.
- **Kodiak pancakes with Nurri** (Nov 2026). Fine. See the almond and oat flour idea above.
- **Birria hash with eggs** (Oct 2026). Good use of leftovers, not its own recipe.

## Didn't work

- **Keema palak** (Oct 2026). The saag chicken method with 1 lb 93/7 ground beef in place of
  the chicken. Just okay on the night, and it smelled terrible out of the fridge the next day,
  bad enough to toss. Don't repeat as written.
