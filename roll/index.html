    Background

    What flavor is made of

    Standard sensory science. The dice score taste, heat, two textures and two aromas because those are the parts an ingredient list can tell you about.

    Taste
    What the tongue registers: salty, sour, sweet, bitter and umami. Five, and that's the whole list.
    Aroma
    Most of what gets called taste is smell. Cumin, basil, smoke and browned butter are all aroma, and aroma is what tells you which cuisine you're in.
    Texture
    Creamy, crunchy, chewy, juicy. A dish with one texture gets boring by the third bite.
    Physical
    Sensations that are neither taste nor smell: chile heat, the tingle of mustard, the cool of mint, temperature.
    Sight
    Color, char and how it sits on the plate set the expectation before the first bite.
    The person
    Memory, mood and what you grew up eating. The same plate is a different dish to two people.
