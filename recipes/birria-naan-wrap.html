Birria Naan Wrap

Leftover birria folded into an H-E-B naan that's been fried in skimmed birria fat, with Oaxaca cheese, onion, cilantro, lime and two creamy salsas. Gyro shape, taco everything else.

Mexican · Beef · High Protein · Quick · Comfort Food

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Melt the birria fat in a nonstick pan over medium heat and cook the naan until it picks up color and the red fat on both sides.
  2. In a second pan, reheat the shredded birria until the edges crisp a little. Lay the Oaxaca cheese on top and let it melt.
  3. Move the naan to a plate. Pile the meat and cheese down the middle, then top with onion, cilantro and a squeeze of lime.
  4. Drizzle both salsas over the top, fold it like a gyro, and wrap the bottom in foil or parchment to hold it together.
Cook's notes

This is the best use of the leftover meat and the skimmed fat. The creamy salsas came from Nuevo León (the restaurant); they're oil-emulsified, so count roughly 60 cal per packet. A Mission fiber tortilla instead of naan cuts about 280 cal but you lose the chew. Macros are estimates: naan ~320, fat ~40, beef ~470, cheese ~80, salsas ~120, toppings ~15.
