Pan-Fried Turkey Dumplings

Ground turkey dumplings seasoned with tamari, ginger, and garlic — boiled until they float, then pan-fried crisp in sesame and avocado oil, with a spicy tamari mayo for dipping.

Servings4
Calories / serving488
Protein / serving28g
Total1950 kcal · 112g

Ingredients

  • --- FILLING ---
  • 1 lb 93/7 ground turkey
  • 2 tbsp tamari
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 scallions, finely sliced
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • --- WRAP + FRY ---
  • About 36 square dumpling wrappers
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • Small bowl of water for sealing
  • --- DIPPING SAUCE ---
  • 2 tbsp tamari
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  • 2 tbsp mayo

Steps

  1. Mix turkey, tamari, sesame oil, ginger, garlic, scallions, and white pepper until just sticky. Don't overwork it.
  2. Put a heaping teaspoon of filling in the center of each wrapper. Wet the edges, fold corner to corner, and press out the air as you seal.
  3. Boil in batches in salted water until they float, plus about 1 more minute. Drain well and let the surface steam dry.
  4. Heat avocado and sesame oil in a nonstick or cast iron pan over medium-high. Pan-fry the boiled dumplings until deep golden and crisp on both flat sides, 2 to 3 minutes a side.
  5. Whisk tamari, sriracha, and mayo for the dipping sauce. Serve hot.

Notes

Boil-then-fry means the filling is always cooked through and the wrapper gets properly crisp. Freeze uncooked dumplings on a sheet tray, then bag them — boil straight from frozen and add a minute.
