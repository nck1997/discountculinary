Mexican Chicken Salad Bowl

Big-batch chipotle chicken, black beans, and veg tossed in a skyr chipotle sauce built on the pan fond. Roughly 1 calorie per gram, so portions are easy to back-math.

Servings3
Calories / serving423
Protein / serving45g
Total1270 kcal · 134g

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cooked chipotle chicken, chopped
  • 1/2 red onion, slivered
  • 1 cup mixed frozen vegetables
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • --- SAUCE ---
  • 3/4 cup 0% skyr
  • 20g chipotle mayo
  • Splash of bone broth, to deglaze
  • Arbol garlic hot sauce, to taste
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Sear the chicken in a hot pan until it picks up some color, then move it to a large bowl.
  2. Cook the frozen vegetables in the same pan until heated through and any water has cooked off. Add to the bowl.
  3. Deglaze the pan with a splash of bone broth, scraping up the fond, and reduce to a couple of tablespoons.
  4. Whisk the reduced fond with skyr, chipotle mayo, and hot sauce.
  5. Toss chicken, vegetables, black beans, and red onion with the sauce. Season and chill, or eat warm.

Notes

Full batch is about 1,270 calories, 134g protein, and 30g fiber — about 1 calorie and 0.1g protein per gram, so you can weigh what's left and know exactly what you ate. Good in a tortilla, over potatoes, or straight from the container.
