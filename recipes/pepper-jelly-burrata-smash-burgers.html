Pepper Jelly Burrata Double Smash Burgers

Double smash patties from 93/7 beef on sesame potato rolls spread with burrata and hot pepper jelly, with smoked Havarti melted between the patties and a pile of pickles. Sweet heat, cream and smoke on a lean burger.

Beef · High Protein · Dinner · Quick · Comfort Food

Makes 2 servings. Whole batch: 1420 kcal, 125g protein.

Steps

  1. Get a cast iron pan very hot and give it a light spray of avocado oil.
  2. Set a beef ball in the pan and smash it hard and thin. Salt the top, let the crust form, then scrape under it and flip. Cook briefly on the second side and pull it. Repeat for all four patties.
  3. Spread burrata on one half of each roll and pepper jelly on the other.
  4. Stack two patties per roll with the shredded Havarti between them so the heat of the patties melts it.
  5. Top with a lot of pickles and close it up.
Cook's notes

One of the best things to come out of this kitchen. The idea came from an H-E-B sample of pepper jelly and cream cheese on a cracker; burrata stands in for the cream cheese. If the pan has room, put the Havarti on the patties and steam it under a lid; sandwiching it between hot patties works too. The jelly is about 35 cal per tablespoon, nearly all sugar, so a tablespoon per burger is plenty. Macros are for both burgers: ~84g carbs, ~62g fat, ~5g fiber.
