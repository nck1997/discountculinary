Fond-Rescue Black Bean Soup

Zero-waste blended black bean soup built on the onion-and-beef fond left in the pan, with beef bone broth, smoked salt, a splash of cream, and cheddar.

Servings4
Calories / serving312
Protein / serving22g
Total1250 kcal · 86g

Ingredients

  • Pan with leftover onion-and-beef fond (from smash burgers, taco beef, or seared steak)
  • 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 3 cups beef bone broth
  • 1 tsp roasted garlic granules
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Smoked sea salt, to taste
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 2 oz sharp cheddar, shredded

Steps

  1. Put the fond pan back over medium heat. Pour in about 1 cup of the bone broth and scrape up every browned bit.
  2. Add black beans, remaining broth, garlic granules, oregano, and basil. Simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Blend until smooth with an immersion blender, or leave it a little chunky if you prefer.
  4. Stir in the cream, season with smoked sea salt, and simmer 2 to 3 more minutes.
  5. Ladle into bowls and top with cheddar.

Notes

Doubles as a chili base: stir in seasoned beef mince and a can of diced tomatoes and simmer until thick. The natural follow-up to a smash slider night.
