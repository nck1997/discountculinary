Everyday Mustard Smash Double

The house burger: 93/7 beef smashed into a scoop of mustard on cast iron, doubled up with cheese on a Martin's potato roll, with pickles and a quick mayo-ketchup-Worcestershire burger sauce.

Beef · High Protein · Dinner · Quick · Comfort Food

Per serving

685kcal

60gprotein

Makes 2 servings. Whole batch: 1370 kcal, 120g protein.

Build a meal around this

Ingredients

  • Burgers
  • Burger Sauce

Steps

  1. Sauce: stir together the mayo, ketchup, Worcestershire, garlic powder, onion powder and salt. Crack in black pepper and stir once more.
  2. Heat a cast iron pan until very hot. Add a light spray of avocado oil and a scoop of mustard where the patty will go.
  3. Set a beef ball on the mustard and smash it thin. Salt the top and let it get a good sear.
  4. Flip, add cheese, and steam it for a few seconds with a splash of water under a lid. Pull the patty and repeat with the rest.
  5. Build: sauce on the roll, two patties, a pile of pickles.
Cook's notes

Lean beef works here because a smash burger is about crust, and the mustard sears into the patty. Shredded cheddar melts fine with the steam even without the emulsifying salts in American cheese. Macros are estimates for two doubles with about a tablespoon of sauce each. For a richer version, see the pepper jelly burrata burger; a cottage cheese pimento spread in place of the cheese is on the to-try list.
